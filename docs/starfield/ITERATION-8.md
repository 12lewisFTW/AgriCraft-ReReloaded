# Iteration 8: growth and gain genes

Date: 8 October 2026. Target version: official Starfield Pastoral `0.6.3fix1`, commit `df2151c66a2a9c02855e4db23108e57c422ce78e`.

## Balance rules

The expressed attribute of the standard `AgriGenome` is used; the second allele is retained for inheritance. The limits are taken from the configuration of the corresponding existing AgriCraft characteristic. The overall clean module `NativeCropGeneRules` contains no references to Starfield, the gameplay executor, or random rolls.

For characteristic `v` with limits `min` and `max`:

```text
t = clamp((v - min) / (max - min), 0, 1)
when max <= min: t = 0
positive duration D: D' = max(1, ceil(D * (1 - 0.5 * t_growth)))
count N in the primary native stack: N' = floor(N * (1 + t_gain))
```

The minimum accurately preserves native behavior. Maximum cuts positive terms to half, rounded up, and doubles core products. Zero phases and native service sentinel `99999` retain the original value. Small terms and quantities have steps due to integer rounding; increasing a trait does not have to change every single phase or stack of one item.

The custom range 2–22 gives `t=0.5` when set to 12; matching limits disable the bonus. The difference calculation uses `double` and does not overflow at the extreme values of `int`.

##Growth and Fertilizers

Starfield remains the sole owner of daily growth, watering, seasonal rules, phases, historical days and regrowth. The narrow optional mixin gets the result of the native `applySpeedGroToPhaseDays`, then applies the gene to this fresh array. The total array of initial phases does not change. The bonus does not subtract days from saved progress and does not turn irrigation/random tick into a calendar day.

Order: initial phases → native fertilizer/profession/additional acceleration of the species → growth gene → native sentinel and executor. Native fertilizer can already reduce the phase to zero; such a zero is retained. With the same genome, the timing in the garden bed and with sidecar-crop sticks coincides. The duration shown by magnifying glass/Jade/TOP is calculated using the same formula.

For example, cauliflower has `[1,2,4,4,1]`, that is, 12 watered days. The maximum gene gives `[1,1,2,2,1]`, that is, 7 days. Deluxe Speed-Gro initially gives `[1,1,3,3,1]`; with the maximum genome the total is also 7 days due to rounding. Bonuses do not add up as two independent subtractions from the original term.

A repeat crop receives genetic term once upon successful transition to regrow. The saved native counter is then decremented in the normal way: corn at maximum requires 2 watered days instead of 4 and does not allow instant re-harvesting. Native Speed-Gro does not shorten regrow in this version; this rule has been retained. Reading a hint, transferring a plant, and loading a save will not reduce the counter again.

## Gain and rollback

The bonus is applied within an existing hand/scythe transaction after a successful native plant change, before the items are published. The main harvest area is limited to the basic `spawnHarvestDrops`: native quality, random quantity, luck, coloring and components are first generated by the original algorithm. Each of its stacks receives a multiplier once, with the components of that particular stack being preserved.

Wheat hay and individual sunflower seeds retain their native quantity and probability; they are outside the realm of core products. Repeated clicks, tool/permission failure, decoratively placed flowers, and normal plant destruction do not receive the bonus. The amount of experience is not multiplied, and additional quality/luck rolls are not made.

If the total exceeds the item's limit, it is split into separate stacks. For example, 63 items at maximum give 64 + 62 with the same components; entities have different UUIDs and retain native motion, age, owner, and pick-up latency. Additional entities use a move-ready constructor and do not consume world random numbers. Failure of any stack, including an additional one, deletes already published parts and restores the plant/genome/progress/experience using the previous rollback mechanism.

## Stage boundaries

Strength and resistance remain the task of iteration 9. The extended tooltip EN/RU reports exactly these two remaining effects. Model/game staging of crop sticks, full processing of special crops, automation and giants remain the next steps.

Four types of wild seeds receive a calendar speedup before becoming a native forage: a base of 7 days, with a maximum of 4. forage itself is a different block, and collecting forage does not yet receive the gain gene. The existing lifecycle removes the sidecar on this native transformation; transfer of genome/crop sticks to a special form is considered in iteration 10.

Real Junimo collection uses the regrow genetic term, but the issuance of products through a separate callback automation is not yet included in the gain transaction. Preview/simulation doesn't increase anything. Native destruction/forage/giant have separate distribution paths and retain the original quantities for now. The graphical client and crop sticks model have not been manually verified. AgriCraft version has not been upgraded and publication has not been completed.
