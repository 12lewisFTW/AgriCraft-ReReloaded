# AgriCraft ReReloaded player wiki

Welcome to the English player guide for **AgriCraft ReReloaded 4.0.20**, Minecraft **1.21.1**. Find a mod, plan its farm, compare gene bonuses and check the exact support limits for your loader.

The six conversion packs are included in both AgriCraft builds; each still requires an appropriate source-mod build. **Native Starfield is NeoForge-only and requires 0.6.3fix1.** Its daily Growth and Gain rules differ from converted crops.

## Automate and inspect

Use [Create](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/Create), [Industrial Foregoing Plant Gatherer](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/Industrial-Foregoing) or [PneumaticCraft drones](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/PneumaticCraft) for documented NeoForge harvesting routes. Their articles explain readiness, output collection and the native Starfield boundaries.

For information, use [JEI](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/JEI) / [EMI](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/EMI) for ordinary crop planning and [Jade](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/Jade), [WTHIT](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/WTHIT) or [The One Probe](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/The-One-Probe) for their supported live inspection views. [Resourceful Config](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/Resourceful-Config) and [Mod Menu](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/Mod-Menu) explain settings access.

## Check a feature before depending on it

The current [Serene Seasons](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/Serene-Seasons) and [Fabric Seasons](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/Fabric-Seasons) adapters do not enforce the external crop calendars. [Botania](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/Botania) has no dedicated shipped crop pack and [REI](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/REI) has no dedicated crop plugin in this release. Their status pages explain what is actually available.

Read [About These Guides](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/About-These-Guides) for sources and verification scope. Server datapacks and settings can change default balances.
