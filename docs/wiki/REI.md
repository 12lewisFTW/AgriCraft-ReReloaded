# REI: no dedicated AgriCraft recipe plugin in 4.0.20

**AgriCraft 4.0.20 has no implemented dedicated REI crop-information plugin.** Commented dependency references in the project are not a working recipe-viewer module.

## Information you can rely on

The release supplies four AgriCraft crop categories through [JEI](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/JEI) and [EMI](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/EMI): produce, clipping, species mutations and growing requirements. Choose the appropriate supported viewer for your loader if you want those custom crop-planning pages.

REI or another mod may still show ordinary crafting recipes in a modpack. That does not imply the AgriCraft custom categories, complete genomes or native Starfield daily progress have been registered there by this release.

## Balance and alternatives

A recipe viewer is an information tool, not a farming buff. Switching viewers does not change Gain, breeding chance or crop maturity. Without the custom categories, use this wiki's crop catalogs and the in-game journal, analyzer and magnifying glass. [Jade](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/Jade), [WTHIT](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/WTHIT) and [The One Probe](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/The-One-Probe) provide their own documented live inspection views.

Future or third-party REI plugins should document their own supported features. See [Compatibility Overview](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/Compatibility-Overview) for current platform and module scope.
