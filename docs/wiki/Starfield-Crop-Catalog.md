# Starfield native crop catalog

All **50 supported native seed types** for Starfield Pastoral **0.6.3fix1**, NeoForge, with AgriCraft **4.0.20**. Read [Starfield Pastoral](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/Starfield-Pastoral) for gene bonuses and the distinction between native and converted crops.

Phase sequences and regrowth below are **native baseline days**, before fertilizer, professions and AgriCraft Growth. Apply Growth's rounding to each eligible duration. Successful watered days and native access rules determine progress; summing phase numbers is not an unconditional real-time forecast. Fall is Starfield's autumn season. Native greenhouse and special seasonal exceptions can affect the listed normal seasons.

## Special cases

- `stardewcraft:cactus_seeds`: All seasons; planting only indoors/in a greenhouse or on the island.
- `stardewcraft:coffee_bean`: Produce also serves as seed; multiple beans per harvest.
- `stardewcraft:fall_seeds`: Replaced by a seasonal ForageBlock at maturity.
- `stardewcraft:fiber_seeds`: Grows without watering; all seasons; normal quality; special drop composition.
- `stardewcraft:grape_seeds`: Trellis; the seed maker returns summer wild seeds.
- `stardewcraft:potato_seeds`: Additional native drops; 20% chance with repeat rolls.
- `stardewcraft:qi_bean`: Normal quality; four one-day phases.
- `stardewcraft:rice_shoot`: Near water (radius 3): automatic watering and +25% growth speed; special geometry.
- `stardewcraft:spring_seeds`: Replaced by a seasonal ForageBlock at maturity.
- `stardewcraft:summer_seeds`: Replaced by a seasonal ForageBlock at maturity.
- `stardewcraft:sunflower_seeds`: Additional seeds on harvest.
- `stardewcraft:taro_tuber`: Near water: automatic watering and faster growth; special geometry.
- `stardewcraft:winter_seeds`: Replaced by a seasonal ForageBlock at maturity.

Wild seeds transform to seasonal forage and retain the planted genome; natural forage does not receive one. Giant eligibility does not guarantee formation. The native 3×3 conditions and daily chance still apply.
