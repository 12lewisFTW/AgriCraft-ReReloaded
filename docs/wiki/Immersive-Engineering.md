# Immersive Engineering: hemp for fiber production

This crop pack adds **one AgriCraft species: hemp**, plus one mutation recipe. It is packaged in both AgriCraft builds; usable gameplay still requires an appropriate Immersive Engineering build for the chosen loader.

## Set up a fiber bed

Convert `immersiveengineering:seed` through the analyzer and plant the AgriCraft seed on single sticks. Alternatively, breed Minecraft pumpkin and wheat using the supplied hemp mutation recipe. Its raw recipe evaluation chance is 50%; both parents must first pass the ordinary breeding checks.

Hemp wants wet, slightly acidic, high-or-better nutrient soil and light 10–16 before Strength tolerance. Its model grows to **two blocks tall** at maturity. It has eight stages, base growth chance 0.75, Growth bonus 0.025, and returns to **stage 4** after harvesting.

## Fiber balance

Each produce trial has an **85% chance of 1–2 hemp fiber**. Average yield per trial is 1.275. Default Gain 1 gives one trial, expected 1.275 fiber; Gain 9–10 gives four trials, expected 5.1 fiber. Failed rolls can make an individual harvest smaller, including zero output.

This gives breeding a role in material throughput while retaining native hemp fiber as the output. Partial stage reset permits repeated harvesting of the established planting. One-parent cloning is allowed with the usual 10% spread roll after selection.

## Scope

The output works wherever ordinary native hemp fiber is accepted. This module does **not** supply a Garden Cloche recipe or a dedicated adapter for Immersive Engineering farming machines. A compatible crop item does not by itself establish machine support for AgriCraft seeds or crop blocks. Use one of the documented [harvesting modules](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/Compatibility-Overview) for crop-stick automation.

See [the crop and mutation reference](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/Immersive-Engineering-Catalog) and [Genes and Balance](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/Genes-and-Balance).
