# Pam's HarvestCraft 2 Crops: diverse soils and mutation paths

The pack contains **97 crop definitions and 97 species mutation recipes** for the **Crops** module. It does not cover HarvestCraft fruit trees or redesign the Food Core kitchen. Data is included in both AgriCraft builds; a compatible source-mod build and any required recipe parents are still needed.

## Growing and harvesting

Convert a supported native seed through the analyzer, then plant the AgriCraft seed on single sticks. All definitions use base growth chance 0.75, Growth bonus 0.025, and reset to **stage 4** after ordinary harvest. One-parent cloning is allowed with the 10% spread roll after selection.

Humidity, acidity and nutrient targets differ across species. Arid plants and wet crops should not be treated as one uniform field. Agave, for example, targets arid, neutral soil with low-or-better nutrients and light 10–16 before tolerance. Strength widens the authored tolerances; it does not replace the need to inspect conditions.

## Balance and variety

Many species produce **1–5 items with a 90% chance per trial**. For that product pattern, Gain 1 averages 2.7 and Gain 9–10 averages 10.8. Check the [full catalog](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/Pams-HarvestCraft-2-Crops-Catalog) for each crop's actual product list, range and chance rather than applying that example universally.

The broad mutation graph gives breeders a way to discover species using particular parent combinations. For example, Minecraft cactus and Pam's pineapple are the parents of agave, with raw recipe chance 50%. That recipe still requires eligible parents, an empty breeding target and the actual species data to be loaded. Some paths depend on plants from other compatibility packs.

The resulting native food items remain usable in their original recipes. AgriCraft's contribution is the cultivation and breeding loop: you can specialize beds by environment, improve a useful genome, then expand production without changing the source kitchen's recipe costs.

The catalog lists all 97 species, their seed and product IDs, exact environmental targets and all supplied mutation pairs. See [Crop Packs and Seed Conversion](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/wiki/Crop-Packs-and-Seed-Conversion) for activation and the status of external season adapters.
