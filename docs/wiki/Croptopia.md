# Croptopia: food crops with AgriCraft genetics

The built-in pack covers **58 farmland crops**. It is included in both AgriCraft release builds and activates automatically with Croptopia when automatic packs are enabled. Use the source mod's appropriate loader build; fruit trees are outside this pack.

## Farming loop

Obtain a native crop seed, convert it through the analyzer, and plant it on single sticks. The resulting crop keeps the recognizable Croptopia produce and AgriCraft controls its growth and genes. Harvesting resets the living plant to **stage 0 of eight stages**. No replacement seed is spent for this ordinary harvest; the whole growth cycle must run again.

All 58 definitions use wet, slightly acidic, high-or-better nutrient soil and light **9–16** before Strength tolerance. They share base growth chance 0.75 and Growth bonus 0.025. Most mature models are one block tall; corn reaches two blocks. Leave room for the model and for harvesting equipment.

## Yield and progression

Every produce trial returns **1–3** of the native crop item with 100% chance. At Gain 1, this means 1–3 per harvest, average 2. At Gain 9–10, four independent trials give 4–12, average 8. These are output ranges, not an eight-item guarantee.

The design rewards a productive genome while keeping food tied to a full regrowing cycle. Good soil and Growth shorten that cycle through eligible growth rolls. Source food recipes retain their original ingredients and effects.

## Breeding boundaries

The pack supplies **no new species mutation recipes**. Same-species two-parent breeding can improve all six stats, and one-parent cloning is allowed with a 10% spread roll after parent selection. Combining two different Croptopia vegetables does not introduce an undocumented third vegetable.

There is no native daily calendar, Starfield quality system or fruit-tree conversion in this module. Seasonal tags in these definitions cover all four seasons.
