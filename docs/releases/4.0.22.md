## AgriCraft Re-Reloaded 1.21.1-4.0.22

### Changed

- Redesigned wooden, iron and obsidian crop sticks, including their block models, textures, item icons and particle textures. This release uses the community contribution with further maintainer artwork revisions.
- Updated eight crafting recipes: plant clippers, wooden and iron hand rakes, hollow irrigation channels, magnifying glasses, seed analyzers, sprinklers and gardening trowels.
- Hollow irrigation channels now use six planks and three ordinary channels to produce three hollow channels.
- Magnifying glasses now use one wooden rod and one glass pane. Gardening trowels use one iron ingot, string and a wooden rod. Both recipes fit the 2×2 inventory crafting grid.
- Hand rakes now use diagonal 3×3 recipes. Wooden rakes use wooden crop sticks in place of a fence; both rakes use two wooden rods.
- Plant clippers now require two iron ingots. Seed analyzers use a smooth stone slab and a base of three wooden slabs. Sprinklers use two iron bars and an iron ingot with the bucket and plank.

### Fixed

- Selection and physical collision shapes now follow the revised crop-stick models, including shifted crossbars and the buried depth of the vertical posts.
