## AgriCraft Re-Reloaded 1.21.1-4.0.21

### Changed

- Updated the sprinkler model and texture with the community contribution from [12lewisFTW](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/issues/3), including its body, rotating blades and inventory appearance.
- Sprinkler blades now use a resource-pack model and its texture coordinates on both NeoForge and Fabric.

### Fixed

- Fixed offhand clippers triggering ordinary harvesting instead of plant clipping. Clippers now work in either hand, with or without sneaking.
- Preserved ordinary empty-handed harvesting and main-hand planting, fertilizer and rake actions. Clippers leave immature plants and empty crop sticks unchanged.

### Downloads

- `AgriCraft-neoforge-1.21.1-4.0.21.jar` — Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge.
- `AgriCraft-fabric-1.21.1-4.0.21.jar` — Minecraft 1.21.1 with Fabric.

### Validation

Both release builds compiled successfully. The final NeoForge JAR passed 39/39 scripted interactions through a real client and dedicated server; the sprinkler body, blade and inventory models loaded with the new texture. All six release/source/development archives passed isolation checks. See [release verification](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/blob/4.0.21/docs/releases/4.0.21-VALIDATION.md) for evidence and testing limits.
